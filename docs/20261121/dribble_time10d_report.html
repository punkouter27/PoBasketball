Gate success by checkpoint

Each bar is 200 gate episodes. The big step at 400 M follows the training horizon going from 25 s to 38 s. After that it holds between 57 % and 71 %; ckpt_600M is likely the lucky end of that band.

Why gate episodes end

Share of the 200 episodes ended by each rule (an episode can trip more than one). Drift, meaning the pelvis wandering more than 0.75 m from where she started, falls with every horizon step. Falls fade out after 250 M.

Training: episode length against the horizon

Training episodes end at the horizon (time-out) or at a gate loss rule. The horizon grows ×1.5 once 60 % of episodes reach it. The actor was frozen for the first 39 M steps while the critic learned; the dip after that is PPO settling in, and it recovered by 120 M.
