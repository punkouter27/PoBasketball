The court got a neighbourhood

Court and hoops

Substrate materials, court wear, and hoops that now stand inside the fence.

What changed

  • Substrate One Substrate material per part: asphalt, leather ball with seams (blended with a dust slab), rim paint chipped down to steel, backboard marks, painted steel with rust flecks, chain-link mesh.
  • Court wear Rubber scuffs under both hoops, paint that fades with the asphalt grain, hairline cracks. Puddles were removed from the playing surface at your request.
  • Hoop poles The poles stood at x = ±16.2 m, 20 cm outside the ±16 m fence. They are now at ±15.0 m, 1 m behind the baseline, like a fenced outdoor court. Only the poles and arms moved; rims, boards, fence, floor and ball are byte-identical, so training contacts are unchanged.

Neighbourhood

A procedural city block outside the fence (PCG), built from stand-in props.

How it is built

  • PCG One graph, PCG_Neighbourhood, with nine branches: brownstone rows facing the court, sidewalk and street strips, trees, street lights, benches / trash cans / hydrants / a fountain, parked cars, weeds along the fence and litter. Each branch thins its anchors randomly by seed, jitters position, yaw and scale, then picks meshes by weight.
  • Megascans stand-ins 18 meshes generated in Blender. Swapping in Fab or Megascans assets means listing them in Content/Art/env_slots.json and re-running build_environment.py.
  • Everything here has collision off and is not in the physics scene.

Grandma

Strand hair, a cloth hem and Substrate skin on the same physics-driven body.

  • Groom 5,326 flyaway strands over the modelled hair plus 300 eyebrow strands, with hair physics on the flyaways. They ride on the head bone.
  • Chaos Cloth The cardigan's lower panel is its own cloth section (3,997 faces), pinned at the seams and free to swing up to 6 cm. Cloth gets its own gravity because the project's world gravity is zero (MuJoCo owns gravity).
  • Substrate Skin texels get subsurface scattering and the white knit gets a fuzz sheen, both picked from the character's own colour map.
  • MetaHuman Not made yet: MetaHuman Creator needs your Epic sign-in. Any body on the UE5 skeleton can be dropped in with -StreetballCharacterMesh=….

Voice and sound

Baked trash talk with lip-sync, plus MetaSounds that react to impact speed.

scramble_start · determinedLoose ball! Out of my way!
scramble_save · smugGotcha! Nobody escapes Grandma.
fall · smugJust checking the floor. It's still there.
make · happyBuckets! Put that on the fridge.
swish · smugNothing but net, honey!
miss · annoyedWho moved the hoop? Somebody moved the hoop.
  • ACE / Audio2Face (offline bake) 45 lines over 10 game events, spoken by the offline Windows voice and shaped to sound older. Mouth shapes come from the audio's loudness and spectrum, with a mood layer (smile, brow drop, squint) and blinks. No Audio2Face runtime is installed on this machine; the bake already reads Audio2Face exports with --a2f-csv when you have them.
  • In game, a line plays at most every 3.5 s, never the same line twice in a row, and the subtitle shows on the HUD. The 68 s capture run played 10 lines.
  • MetaSounds Ball, palm, shoe, rim, board and net sounds take an Impact input: soft dribbles sound muffled, hard pounds bright and boomy. Also a crowd cheer and groan, an effort grunt, and a looping city bed with traffic, birds and a distant siren.
